# Overview

**Life OS** is a personal productivity system designed to help ambitious people turn their intentions into meaningful action. It aims to bridge the gap between **knowing what needs to be done and actually getting it done**, helping people manage commitments, focus on what matters, and make consistent progress toward their goals.

You have goals. You make plans. You know what needs to be done.

## The execution gap

We often know what we need to do, but **knowing doesn't always lead to action**. Ideas appear in the middle of other tasks, commitments get buried, and to-do lists keep growing. As everything starts to feel important, **deciding what deserves attention becomes a task in itself**.

This is made worse by **vague goals, unrealistic plans, constant distractions, and the difficulty of getting started**. People can spend an entire day feeling busy without making progress on what truly matters. Over time, this creates a cycle of postponement, mental overload, and frustration, widening the gap between **what people intend to achieve and what they actually accomplish**.

**Scattered Intentions → Overwhelming Priorities → Delayed Action → Stalled Progress**

## Information Architecture

### Today ☀️
- **Daily OS**: Capacity vs Demand Meter, Focus Minutes Allowance, Fixed Meetings Union, Active Day Plan
- **Repair Engine**: Disruption Detection, Deficit Calculation, Repair Proposal Card (Accept, Undo / Revert, Dismiss), Staged Deferrals
- **Quick Capture**: Frictionless Input, Natural Language Parser, Firm vs Flexible Toggle, Duration Guess
- **Close of Day**: Evening Closure Card, Keep / Defer / Drop, Tomorrow Carryover, Zero-Guilt State

### Tasks & Workflows ✅
- **Inbox**: Unprocessed Items, Quick Triage, Source Metadata
- **Active Backlog**: Firm Commitments, Flexible Tasks, Context & Energy Tags, Deadline Defenses
- **Workflows**: Kanban Boards, Project Milestones, Stage Progression
- **Matrix Views**: Eisenhower 2×2, Effort vs Impact, Time Estimation Accuracy

### Calendar & Schedule 🗓️
- **Unified Timeline**: 24-Hour Day Strip, Google & Outlook Sync, Meeting Union Blocks
- **Capacity Defense**: Focus Block Armor, Transition Reserve (90m), Minimum Feasible Slot Filter
- **Event Manager**: Fixed Appointments, Overrun Resolver (Dynamic Recompute, Buffer Absorption), Timezone Overlap Guard

### Focus & Habits 🎯
- **Deep Work**: Distraction-Free Timer, Binaural Soundscapes, Post-Session Wrap (Completed, Continue 15m, Mark Blocked)
- **Habit Engine**: Morning & Evening Loops, Protected Habit Slots, Streak Continuity
- **Analytics**: Usable Focus Trends, Context-Switching Cost, Weekly Velocity

### AI Companion ✨
- **Assistant**: Re-planning Dialogue, Reasoning Explanations, Command Bar (Cmd+K)
- **Control & Settings**: Transparent Suggestions, Non-Destructive Undo, Integrations (Calendar, Slack)
