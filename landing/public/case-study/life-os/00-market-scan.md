# Market Scan: AI Daily Planners (fetched 11 Oct 2026)

Method: pricing pages fetched directly, because web search was blocked in this environment. Prices are list prices in USD on the day of fetch. This is competitor reality from their own pages, not user satisfaction data.

## Fact: what each tool sells

| Tool | Entry price | What it is | Trial |
|---|---|---|---|
| Motion | $19/seat/mo (Pro AI), $29 (Business AI) | AI task planner, calendar, projects, docs. Credit-capped: 7,500 AI credits/seat/mo on Pro | Free trial, length not stated |
| Sunsama | $17/mo yearly, $22 monthly | Daily planning ritual, Google/Outlook calendar, AI + MCP | 14 days, no card |
| Reclaim | Free (Lite), $10 Starter, $15 Business (yearly) | Habits, task time-blocking, focus-time defense, scheduling links | Free tier |
| Akiflow | $19/mo yearly, $34 monthly | Time blocking, task aggregation, daily planning ritual, Aki AI assistant | 7 days, card required |

## Inference

- Every tool above treats planning as a calendar problem. **Correction (11 Oct):** I first wrote that none of them mention shutdown closure. That came from pricing pages only. Their feature pages show Sunsama and Akiflow name shutdown rituals, Motion re-plans when meetings change, and Akiflow has a "replan undone tasks" button. What none of the pages describe is weighing what carries over against remaining capacity, or explaining why something moved.
- Prices run $10 to $34 a month, in USD. For an India-first user that is a real cost barrier. Akiflow says it may use purchasing-power pricing in some countries, but gives no numbers.
- Motion meters AI by credits. Users who want AI to rearrange their day pay per action, which cuts against the calm, predictable feel the HMW asks for.
- Reclaim's free tier means a cost-sensitive user can already get habits plus time-blocking at no charge. A new entrant cannot win on price.

## Caveats (contrarian evidence)

- Sunsama's own FAQ says $25/$20 while its plan card says $22/$17. The page contradicts itself.
- Motion's page did not say which price belongs to monthly versus annual billing.
- Reclaim's cards and comparison table disagree on Starter calendar-sync limits.
- Morgen could not be fetched (domain did not resolve). Not covered.
- No page here measures retention or satisfaction, so none of this shows users are unhappy with these tools.

## Recommendation

Do not position on features or price. Re-planning and shutdown rituals already ship. Position on effort and explainability: what still fits, what carries over, why something moved, and what only the user knows is firm. Test that against satisfied users of these four tools.
